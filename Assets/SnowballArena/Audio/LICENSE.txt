Background music (OpenGameArt, CC0 1.0)
--------------------------------------
  Music/menu_theme.ogg   <- "Swinging Sweet", from "Short Loops Background Music Pack" by hernandack
  Music/match_theme.ogg  <- "A Brand New Wisdom", same pack by hernandack
  https://opengameart.org/content/short-loops-background-music-pack

  The first match track tried was "rememberWinter" (Short Loop: winter theme by yd),
  but it measures ~25 dB RMS while the menu track sits at ~13 dB, so it was replaced
  with a track from the same loudness family (-14 dB) to keep the menu/match switch
  seamless. This track is 16.6 s so it loops more often than the 24.9 s menu track.

Cannon shot and hit (OpenGameArt, CC0 1.0)
------------------------------------------
  Cannon/cannon_fire.wav        <- "Cannon fire" by Thimras, trimmed to 1.20 s
  https://opengameart.org/content/cannon-fire
  Real recording. Only trimming, mono fold-down, a fade-out and peak
  normalisation were applied. Earlier revisions synthesised this in the
  project, but a generated "boom" kept reading as a firework: it needs a real
  low-frequency report with a fast decay, which synthesised noise could not fake.

Explosion (OpenGameArt, CC-BY 3.0)
----------------------------------
  Cannon/cannon_explosion.wav   <- "2 High Quality Explosions" by Michel Baradari,
                                   first blast only, trimmed to 1.55 s
  https://opengameart.org/content/2-high-quality-explosions
  Attribution required (CC-BY 3.0). The file packs two blasts; only the first
  was used. Earlier revisions used "Cannon hit wall" (CC0), but that is an
  impact-plus-debris sound, not the sustained boom the hit needed.
